Changelog

1.0.0

Changes from 6.5.0

  • Removed the Pig Challenge completely, including it's banners and pop-ups in normal gameplay.
  • Restored the bird launch, collision and ability sprites from the older versions.
  • Restored score sprites such as the "5000"s or the "100000"s to the older sprites.
  • Removed the Facebook login.
  • The episode selection menu has been redesigned to resemble older versions.
  • Added an unused power-up: Egg Bombs
  • Remade the Telebird graphics to make them higher quality and classic styled.
  • Remade most character graphics for the episodes from 2014 and later to be classic styled.
  • Fixed the Level Results screen crashing in bonus levels.
  • Fixed the Intel egg not appearing in Winter Wonderham.
  • Fixed the camera not cooperating in Wreck the Halls 1-25.
  • Added splashes based on the episode last played.
  • The hud have been modified to resemble the ones in 5.1.0 to 5.2.5 versions.
  • Restored the episode buttons to their older counterparts.
  • Restored the older Power-Up Icons.
  • The Hogs and Kisses and Ham'o'ween logos have been restored back to their original sizes
  • Added all episodes locally.
  • Removed the forced tutorial to Hammier Things.
  • Added music variants to episodes with multiple versions of the title music.
  • Removed the "Last Chance" popup so that the game goes directly to the level failed screen.
  • Restored multiple background and foreground effects.
  • Fixed the "Windthing" (yes, that's what it's called) particles not showing up.
  • Restored the Matilda Ability Sprite.
  • Restored the bats on pumpkins.
  • Removed the requirement to watch an ad to get access to a level used in Quests.
  • Images that have uncompressed PNG quality versions like in the PC versions or from Angry Birds Reloaded have been added replacing the compressed graphics.
  • Made Mighty Eagle unlocked from the start.
  • Made the camera more zoomed out for ultra wide devices.
  • Added 12 new Bonus Levels on select episodes.
  • Fixed the Ghost Blocks.
  • Removed the coin counter in most areas.
  • Remade all graphics for Tony to be classic styled.
