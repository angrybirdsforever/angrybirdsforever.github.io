Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

Are the games available in 64 bit Android?

Sadly, no. Since the games were always 32 bit at the time, there's not much I can do to make them support 64 bit android.

Are the games merged together?

No, the games are able to be installed separately.

Are the games installed over the original versions from the original Rovio releases?

No, the games install separately.

When is x game coming out?

Whenever the game is complete, it will come out.

Will Angry Birds Go!/Epic/Fight have it's own Forever version?

No, as the "Forever" project focuses on the older slingshot games for now.

Will the games be available on iOS?

Eventually, after Seasons is completed for it's Android beta release, work will start for the iOS ports.

Angry Birds Seasons Questions

Are designs going to be changed to the "Chrome" ones?

No, as the game depends too much on the older designs for them to be replaced
